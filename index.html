简要说明：Killer Sudoku 的游戏进度和个人统计主要保存在您的设备本地。 本应用可能使用 Firebase Analytics 进行不包含姓名、邮箱或完整棋盘内容的应用行为统计， 并使用 Google AdMob 展示广告及 Google 用户消息平台（UMP）管理适用地区的隐私选择。

1. 适用范围

本隐私政策适用于 Android 应用 Killer Sudoku（软件包名称：com.gpg.game0000，以下简称“本应用”）。本政策说明本应用如何处理信息、使用第三方服务，以及您可以作出的隐私选择。

2. 我们处理的信息

保存在设备本地的信息：

  • 游戏进度、关卡完成记录、用时和个人游戏统计；
  • 应用设置、语言、主题、提示偏好和隐私选择状态；
  • 继续游戏所需的本地存档。

上述本地数据通常不会由我们上传到自有服务器。您可以通过清除应用数据或卸载本应用删除这些本地数据。

匿名或假名化的应用分析信息：

本应用可能使用 Google Firebase Analytics 收集应用实例标识符、设备与应用版本信息、大致地区信息（例如由 IP 地址推断的地区）、会话信息以及有限的应用交互事件，例如开始或完成关卡、使用提示和完成教程。这些信息用于了解应用是否正常工作以及改进游戏体验。

我们不会主动向分析事件中加入姓名、电子邮箱、登录令牌、账号 ID、完整棋盘、谜题答案、逐格输入内容或自由文本。Firebase Analytics 使用的应用实例标识符并不等同于完全匿名，因此本政策将此类信息描述为假名化信息。

广告和同意管理信息：

本应用使用 Google AdMob 展示广告，并可能使用 Google 用户消息平台（UMP）请求、保存和传递您的隐私选择。Google 及其广告合作伙伴可能处理广告标识符、IP 地址、设备和应用信息、广告展示与互动信息、诊断信息以及您的同意状态。根据您的地区、选择、设备设置和适用法律，广告可能为个性化广告、非个性化广告或受限广告。

3. 信息的用途

  • 保存和恢复游戏进度、设置及个人统计；
  • 提供、维护、诊断和改进本应用；
  • 统计关卡流程和功能使用情况；
  • 展示广告并衡量广告效果；
  • 记录和遵守适用的隐私选择；
  • 防止欺诈、滥用和安全问题。

4. 第三方服务与信息共享

本应用可能使用 Google Firebase Analytics、Google AdMob 和 Google 用户消息平台（User Messaging Platform，UMP）。为了提供这些服务，相关信息可能发送给 Google 及其服务提供商，并依照其政策在不同国家或地区处理。我们不会出售您的个人信息，也不会将信息分享给第三方用于与本政策所述目的无关的用途。

更多信息请参阅 Google 隐私权政策、Google 如何使用合作伙伴应用中的信息以及 Firebase 隐私与安全信息。

5. 登录、后端与购买

当前公开 MVP 版本不提供登录、云端同步、排行榜、应用内购买或开发者自有后端账号功能。如果未来版本启用其中任何功能，我们会在启用前更新本隐私政策及相应的应用商店披露。

6. 您的选择与控制

  • 在适用地区，您可以在首次显示的隐私消息中选择同意、不同意或管理选项；
  • 您可以通过本应用内的“隐私选项”入口重新查看或修改可用选择；
  • 您可以在 Android 系统设置中重置或删除广告 ID，并管理设备允许的广告隐私设置；
  • 您可以清除本应用的数据或卸载本应用，以删除设备本地保存的数据。

由于本应用的统计与广告数据不与开发者账号关联，我们通常无法仅凭邮箱识别并删除某一特定应用实例在 Google 系统中的全部记录。您仍可通过下方邮箱联系我们提出隐私问题或请求，我们会在合理范围内协助处理。

7. 数据保留与安全

本地游戏数据会保留在您的设备上，直至您清除应用数据或卸载本应用。由 Google 处理的数据将按照我们的服务配置、Google 的保留规则及适用法律进行保存。我们采取合理措施减少收集的信息，并避免在分析事件中发送不必要的个人数据；但任何电子存储或网络传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

8. 儿童隐私

本应用面向一般用户，并非专门面向 13 岁以下儿童（或当地法律规定的相应年龄）。我们不会主动要求用户提供姓名、电子邮箱或账号资料。如果您认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。

9. 政策变更

我们可能因功能、第三方服务或法律要求发生变化而更新本政策。更新后的版本将在此页面发布，并修改页面顶部的生效日期。重大变更将在适当情况下通过应用内提示或其他合理方式告知。

10. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策或信息处理方式有疑问，请联系：rick1999.lab@gmail.com

Summary: Killer Sudoku keeps gameplay progress and personal statistics primarily on your device. The App may use Firebase Analytics for app-usage measurement without sending names, email addresses, or complete puzzle-board contents. It uses Google AdMob for advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable privacy choices.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Killer Sudoku, package name com.gpg.game0000 (the “App”). It explains how information is handled, which third-party services are used, and the privacy choices available to you.

2. Information We Handle

Information stored locally on your device:

  • Gameplay progress, level-completion records, completion times, and personal gameplay statistics;
  • App settings, language, theme, hint preferences, and privacy-choice status;
  • Local saved-game data needed to resume play.

This local information is generally not uploaded to a server operated by us. You can remove it by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

Anonymous or pseudonymous analytics information:

The App may use Google Firebase Analytics to collect an app-instance identifier, device and app-version information, approximate regional information (for example, a region inferred from an IP address), session information, and limited app-interaction events such as starting or completing a level, using a hint, and completing the tutorial. We use this information to understand whether the App works correctly and to improve the gameplay experience.

We do not intentionally add names, email addresses, sign-in tokens, account IDs, complete puzzle boards, puzzle answers, cell-by-cell input, or free-form text to analytics events. Because an app-instance identifier is not the same as fully anonymous data, this Policy describes such information as pseudonymous.

Advertising and consent-management information:

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and may use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request, store, and communicate your privacy choices. Google and its advertising partners may process an advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostic information, and consent status. Depending on your location, choices, device settings, and applicable law, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To save and restore gameplay progress, settings, and personal statistics;
  • To provide, maintain, diagnose, and improve the App;
  • To measure level flow and feature usage;
  • To display ads and measure advertising performance;
  • To record and respect applicable privacy choices;
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues.

4. Third-Party Services and Sharing

The App may use Google Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Information needed to provide these services may be sent to Google and its service providers and may be processed in countries or regions other than your own under their policies. We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for purposes unrelated to those described in this Policy.

For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from partner apps, and Firebase Privacy and Security information.

5. Sign-In, Backend Services, and Purchases

The current public MVP does not provide sign-in, cloud synchronization, leaderboards, in-app purchases, or a developer-operated backend account. If a future version enables any of these features, we will update this Privacy Policy and the relevant app-store disclosures before enabling them.

6. Your Choices and Controls

  • Where applicable, you can consent, decline, or manage options in the privacy message shown by the App;
  • You can revisit available choices through the App's “Privacy options” entry;
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID and manage available advertising privacy settings in Android settings;
  • You can clear the App's data or uninstall the App to remove locally stored information.

Because analytics and advertising information is not associated with a developer account, we generally cannot identify and remove every Google-held record for a particular app instance using an email address alone. You may still contact us with a privacy question or request, and we will provide reasonable assistance.

7. Data Retention and Security

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Information processed by Google is retained according to our service configuration, Google's retention practices, and applicable law. We take reasonable steps to minimize the information collected and avoid sending unnecessary personal data in analytics events; however, no electronic storage or network-transmission method is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction. We do not intentionally ask users to provide a name, email address, or account profile. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the App, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Where appropriate, material changes will also be communicated through an in-app notice or another reasonable method.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or our information-handling practices, contact: rick1999.lab@gmail.com
